eyboards are essential input devices used to type text, numbers, and commands into computers and other devices. Keyboards come in different types, such as mechanical, membrane , and wireless, each offering unique feel and responsiveness. Fog origins

Fog is a low-lying cloud that forms when air temperatures cool to the dew point. This causes invisible water vapor to condense into microscopic, suspended liquid droplets (or ice crystals) on particles like dust or pollen



tips

Navigating through fog requires adjusting your driving habits to prioritize visibility and reaction time. The most critical rules are to slow down significantly, turn on your low-beam headlights (never high beams), and leave a generous following distance between your car and the vehicle ahead
